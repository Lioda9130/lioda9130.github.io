CITY FIELD GUIDE

Lose yourself in the district.

A cyberpunk city built for wandering. Follow a street, watch the traffic, or choose a district on the map.

01 / NIGHT MARKET

Under the neon.

Shopfronts, glowing signs and passing pedestrians line the western streets. Follow the side roads and explore at street level.

02 / NEON PLAZA

A moment between the towers.

An open space beside the boulevard. Look up at the skyline, watch vehicles roll past and find your next turn.

03 / SKYRAIL

The city keeps moving.

Head north toward the elevated railway. Trains cross above the streets while the district carries on below.

EXPLORER CONTROLS

Make your own way.

Drag to look in any direction. Click a road to travel, or walk with WASD and the arrow keys. Hold Shift to move faster. Press Escape to cancel a route and R to reset while the city is focused. Motion pauses ambient activity. On touch screens, drag to look and tap a road to move.
